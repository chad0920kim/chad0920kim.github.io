Privacy Policy

Slide 100 (com.tide.slidepuzzle) · Last updated July 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Slide 100 can be used without any sign-up or login. Game progress data such as stage progress, best records, and star ratings is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to the developer or any third-party server.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app displays banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use device information such as your Advertising ID. You can review how Google collects and uses data at the links below.

  • Google Ads Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier from your device settings.

3. Children's Privacy

Slide 100 is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress data is stored only in local device storage and is deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or use the in-app "Reset Progress" feature.
