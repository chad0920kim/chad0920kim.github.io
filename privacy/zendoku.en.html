Privacy Policy

ZenDoku (com.zendoku.sudoku) · Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy explains how Chad Kim ("the developer") handles personal information of people who use the ZenDoku sudoku app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google account information: ZenDoku uses Google sign-in. When you sign in, the app receives from Google your email address, name, profile picture URL (if set in your Google account), and Google account identifier, and stores them with an in-app user ID. Your Google password is never received.
  • Game records: For completed puzzles, the puzzle type, difficulty, solving time, puzzle number, and completion time; for the puzzle in progress, the cell values, notes, and last saved time.
  • Temporary storage on your device: So you can keep playing offline, the puzzle in progress, completions not yet uploaded, and the last signed-in user ID are stored on your device.

2. How We Use It

  • Signing you in and identifying your account
  • Saving your game records, continuing on other devices, and showing statistics and streaks

We do not sell this information or use it for marketing.

3. Where It Is Stored (Service Providers)

Account information and game records are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), the cloud database and authentication service used by the developer. Supabase servers may be located outside your country. Communication between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS, and access is restricted so that each user can only access their own records. Google's account services are used to process Google sign-in.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows banner and interstitial (full-screen) ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its ad partners may collect and use device information such as the Advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), and app usage and device data. The developer does not share your account information or game records with advertisers.

  • Google Ads policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can limit personalized ads or reset/delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

ZenDoku is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Account information and game records are kept until you delete your account.
  • In the app: Choose "Delete account" in the app's Settings screen to delete your account and all game records.
  • Without the app: Send an email from the email address of the Google account you signed in with to kyh0920@gmail.com with the subject "ZenDoku account deletion request". We will process it and reply within 7 days. See the account and data deletion page for details.
  • Once deletion is processed, your account information and game records are erased immediately and the developer keeps no separate copy.
  • Data temporarily stored on your device is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • You can also remove ZenDoku's access in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps & services). This does not delete records stored in ZenDoku, so please request deletion as described above.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or to access, correct, or delete your personal information, please contact us at the email below.
