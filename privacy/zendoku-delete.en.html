Account and Data Deletion Request

ZenDoku (com.zendoku.sudoku) · Developer: Chad Kim · Last updated November 5, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your ZenDoku account and game data. Please use either of the two methods below.

1. Delete in the app

  • Open the ZenDoku app and sign in.
  • Go to the Settings screen.
  • Tap "Delete account" and confirm.

2. Request by email (if you cannot use the app)

  • Send an email from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in to ZenDoku to kyh0920@gmail.com.
  • Subject: ZenDoku account deletion request
  • Body: the email address of the account to delete
  • To verify your identity, we only process requests sent from the registered email address. If you write from another address, we will send you a confirmation email.
  • We will process the deletion and reply within 7 days of receiving your request.
  • To keep your account but delete only your game records, use the subject "ZenDoku game data deletion request" (kyh0920@gmail.com).

3. Data that is deleted

  • Account information: email address, name, profile picture URL, Google account identifier, in-app user ID
  • All game records: completed puzzle records (type, difficulty, solving time, completion time, etc.) and the puzzle in progress

4. Data that is kept, and for how long

  • Once deletion is processed, the data above is erased immediately and the developer keeps no separate copy.
  • If you requested by email, the request emails may remain in the developer's mailbox as a record that the request was handled.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's policies and is not linked to your ZenDoku account. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Data temporarily stored on your device is erased when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

For details on how personal information is handled, see the ZenDoku Privacy Policy.
