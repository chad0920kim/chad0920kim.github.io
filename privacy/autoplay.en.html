Privacy Policy

AutoPlay (com.chadkim.autoplay) · Last updated November 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect and Use

AutoPlay can be used without any sign-up or login, and the developer does not operate any server. The app stores the following only on your device and never sends it to the developer or any third party:

  • The name and identifier (Bluetooth address) of the Bluetooth devices you select for auto-play
  • App settings such as which music app to start (its package name)
  • When your free trial started and whether you have purchased the app

2. Permissions and Why We Use Them

  • Notification access: Used only to check the playback state (playing/paused) of your chosen music app through the system's media session, so that playback can be resumed automatically when music stops after a call or notification and does not restart by itself. The app does not read the content (title, text, etc.) of other apps' notifications, and does not store or send it. You can turn this permission on or off in system settings; auto-play on Bluetooth connection still works without it.
  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): Used to list paired devices and to detect when your selected devices connect or disconnect. It is not used to determine your location.
  • Battery optimization exemption: Requested so the app does not miss a Bluetooth connection and can start music immediately even when it is in the background or the screen is off. Granting it is your choice and can be changed at any time in device settings.

3. In-App Purchases

After a 7-day free trial, auto-play can be unlocked with a one-time purchase (lifetime). Payments are processed by Google Play's billing system; the developer does not receive card numbers or other payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase is complete.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows banner ads at the bottom of the screen through Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location inferred from your IP address, device information (model, OS version, etc.), and ad impression and click data. This information is handled under Google's policies, and the developer does not receive personally identifiable information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Google > Ads. For details, see Google's advertising policies. Ads are not shown after you buy the lifetime pass.

5. Children's Privacy

AutoPlay is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Device and setting information is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Purchase records are managed by Google Play and can be viewed in your Google account's purchase history.
