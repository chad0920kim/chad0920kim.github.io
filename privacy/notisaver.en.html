Privacy Policy

NotiSaver (com.chadkim.notisaver.notisaver) · Last updated November 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • The app can be used without any sign-up or login, and the developer does not operate any server.
  • Notification records (notification access): If you allow notification access, the app saves the app name, sender/title, message text, and time of notifications from the apps you choose to archive (such as messengers) in a database on your device (SQLite). These records are never sent off your device, including to the developer.
  • Installed apps: The app checks installed apps on your device to find messenger apps you can choose to archive. This information is not sent anywhere.
  • App lock (biometrics): If you turn on app lock, fingerprint or face authentication is handled by the device's operating system (Android), and the app only receives whether authentication succeeded. The app does not access or store biometric data.
  • App settings such as keyword alerts are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its ad partners may collect and use device information such as the Advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), and app usage and device data. The developer does not receive or store this information.

  • Google Ads policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can limit personalized ads or reset/delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are not shown after you purchase ad removal.

3. In-App Purchases

Ad removal is offered as a one-time purchase. Payments are processed by Google Play's billing system; the developer does not receive card numbers or other payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase is complete and stores that result on your device.

4. Children's Privacy

NotiSaver is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved notification records are kept only on your device. You can delete them one by one or all at once in the app, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app. You can turn off notification access at any time in system settings; new notifications are no longer saved once it is off. Purchase records are managed by Google Play.
