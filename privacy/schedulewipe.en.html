Privacy Policy

Schedule Wipe (com.hannune.schedule) · Last updated November 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • The app can be used without any sign-up or login, and the developer does not operate any server.
  • Calendar (read calendar permission): The app reads the calendar events on your device (title, time, etc.) to show and read today's schedule on the lock screen and with alarms. Events are processed only on your device and are never sent elsewhere or stored separately.
  • Voice: Schedules are read aloud using the text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed on your device. The app does not send your schedule text to any external speech server.
  • App settings such as alarm times and display options are stored only on your device.

2. Information Sent Outside Your Device

To include the weather in alarms, the app requests forecast data from the Korea Meteorological Administration short-term forecast API (Public Data Portal, data.go.kr). The request contains only the forecast grid coordinates of a fixed area (Seoul), the forecast base time, and the app's API key; it contains no personal information such as your location, schedule, or device details. As with any internet request, the receiving server can see your IP address.

3. Permissions and Why We Use Them

  • Display over other apps: Used to show today's schedule over the lock screen.
  • Exact alarms / run at startup: Used to ring alarms at the times you set and to re-register them after the device restarts.
  • Battery optimization exemption: Requested so alarms and the lock-screen schedule work on time even in power-saving states; granting it is your choice.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its ad partners may collect and use device information such as the Advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), and app usage and device data. The developer does not receive or store this information.

  • Google Ads policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can limit personalized ads or reset/delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are not shown after you purchase the premium pass.

5. In-App Purchases

Premium features, including ad removal, are offered as a one-time purchase (premium pass). Payments are processed by Google Play's billing system; the developer does not receive card numbers or other payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase is complete.

6. Children's Privacy

Schedule Wipe is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

App settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. The app does not keep its own copy of your calendar events. Purchase records are managed by Google Play.
