We're Recruiting Slide 100 Testers!

Join our closed beta before launch and be among the first to play

Slide 100 is a classic sliding puzzle game — push tiles into the empty space to put them back in order. It features 100 stages across 20 different grid sizes, from 3x3 all the way up to 7x8.

What are we testing?

  • Difficulty and fun across all 100 stages
  • Screen design, including dark mode
  • Overall usability — sound effects, how often ads appear, and more
  • Bugs or crashes

How to join

  1. Tap the "Join as a Tester" button below to sign up for the Play Store testing program
  2. Install Slide 100 from the Play Store (it may take a few minutes to show up)
  3. Play freely, and if you run into any issues or have feedback, just send us an email
